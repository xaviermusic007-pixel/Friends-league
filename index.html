📋 Tournament Rules

  • 1. Game & Device: eFootball Mobile. Every participant plays on their own mobile phone via Friend Match room code.
  • 2. Knockout Format: Single-elimination knockout bracket. Extra Time + Penalties ON for all knockout ties. Semi-final losers play a Third-Place Playoff for the ₹10 prize.
